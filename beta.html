Android beta

Beta testing focus

PhilaVault is currently being tested with a small Android tester group. The beta focuses on album usability, crop quality, hosted AI reliability, and clear review of AI suggestions.

What to test

  • Create an account and sign in.
  • Add a stamp manually.
  • Capture or import a stamp photo.
  • Use crop review, rotation, and precision loupe.
  • Request AI suggestions and review every field before saving.
  • Browse the album by issuer, sort mode, and view mode.
  • Open stamp details and review catalogue leads as research aids.
  • Export the local collection.

Beta limits

AI scan access is limited during beta to protect service cost and quality. Manual cataloging remains available when AI quota is exhausted.
