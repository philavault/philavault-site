Privacy Policy

PhilaVault helps collectors photograph, catalog, and review stamp details in a private digital album. This policy explains what information the Android beta handles and how it is used.

Information the app handles

  • Account information when login is enabled, such as email address and user identifier.
  • Stamp records you create, including name, country or issuing entity, year, denomination, condition, rarity, notes, estimated value range, and stamp image.
  • Stamp photos captured with the device camera.
  • Stamp photos selected from the device photo library when you choose to import an existing image.
  • AI scan requests, when enabled, containing the stamp photo and technical request metadata needed to analyze the image.
  • AI usage records, including account identifier, timestamp, model name, and scan status, used to enforce beta scan limits.
  • Subscription or entitlement status when billing is enabled in a future release.

How information is used

  • Stamp records and copied stamp photos are stored on your device in the current beta.
  • Photos are sent to the hosted PhilaVault AI service only when you request AI-assisted suggestions.
  • AI suggestions are used to prefill editable stamp details. They are not verified catalogue facts until you review and save them.
  • Catalogue leads and match-readiness notes are generated from saved details, visible text, and a small beta reference set. They are research aids, not certified catalogue matches or valuations.
  • Account and entitlement data are used to protect AI scan limits and prevent misuse of the hosted AI service.
  • Successful AI scan events are recorded with your account identifier, timestamp, and AI model name so the beta can enforce scan limits and monitor reliability.

What PhilaVault does not do

  • PhilaVault does not sell personal information.
  • PhilaVault does not use stamp photos for advertising.
  • PhilaVault does not display ads.
  • PhilaVault does not save AI suggestions automatically.
  • PhilaVault does not claim AI suggestions are guaranteed catalogue identifications.
  • PhilaVault does not place an OpenAI API key or backend service-role key inside the mobile app.

Third-party processing

If AI scan is enabled, stamp images are processed by OpenAI through the PhilaVault Cloudflare Worker proxy service. Login is handled by Supabase. Future subscription entitlement checks may use RevenueCat, App Store, or Google Play billing services.

Data control

You can edit, delete, export, or clear local stamp records inside the app. Deleting a stamp removes it from the app collection on the device. During beta, account deletion and server-side AI usage deletion requests can be sent to philavault@gmail.com.

Contact

For privacy or beta support questions, contact philavault@gmail.com.
