  • Your Android device model and Android version.
  • Whether the issue happened on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  • What you were doing when the issue happened.
  • Whether the issue affects manual cataloging, AI scan, crop review, album browsing, or login.

Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your PhilaVault beta account and associated server-side data, email philavault@gmail.com from the same email address used to sign in to PhilaVault. Use the subject line "Delete my PhilaVault account".

Account deletion requests remove the beta login account, server-side AI usage and entitlement records, and AI scan event records associated with that account. Local stamp records are stored on your device in the current beta and can be deleted, exported, or cleared inside the app.

If any records must be retained temporarily for security, abuse prevention, or legal reasons, they will be kept only for as long as needed for that purpose.
